# Pattern: Chronological

Tell the story in the order it happened — but resist a flat timeline recitation.

Structure:
1. **Hook** (0-30s) — a striking moment or number from the disaster itself, not a
   date. Never open with "On [date]...".
2. **Before** — the world, the ship, the people, the decisions in the days/weeks
   leading up to the event. Plant details here that pay off later (a delayed repair,
   an ignored warning, an ordinary passenger with an extraordinary story).
3. **The event** — the disaster itself, told beat by beat with escalating tension.
   This is where most payoff nodes should land.
4. **Aftermath** — rescue, investigation, immediate consequences.
5. **Legacy** — what changed (or didn't) because of it; why it was forgotten; close
   on the angle you were given, not a generic moral.

Use chronology as connective tissue, but let the ANGLE — not the calendar — decide
what to linger on and what to compress.
