# Pattern: Perspective Flip

Alternate between two vantage points on the same event — most often victim/passenger
POV vs. investigator/authority POV — flipping at clear structural beats so each flip
re-contextualizes what came before.

Structure:
1. **Hook** (0-30s) — open inside ONE perspective (usually a passenger or crew
   member) at a vivid, specific moment.
2. **Flip 1** — cut to the other perspective (an investigator, an official, a
   rescuer) covering the same window of time, revealing something the first
   perspective couldn't have known.
3. **Flip 2 (and 3, if length allows)** — continue alternating; each flip should add
   new information, not just repeat the same beat from a different angle.
4. **Convergence** — the two threads meet (the passenger is found/lost, the
   investigation reaches its conclusion) — this is usually the strongest payoff node.
5. **Legacy** — close from whichever perspective best serves the angle.

Label the flips implicitly through content (new scene, new voice, new information),
not with literal "MEANWHILE" narration — trust pacing and the shot list to signal it.
